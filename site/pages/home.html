Everything a position needs

From the first move to the final result.

01 / RULES

Legal by construction

Bitboard move generation, castling, en passant, promotion, checks, and automatic or claimable draws.

02 / NOTATION

Speaks chess

FEN, SAN, UCI coordinates, PGN main lines, and EPD suites. Structured errors for invalid positions and moves.

03 / STATE

State you can trust

Defensive snapshots, independent clones, exact move undo, and repetition-aware position keys.

04 / VARIANTS

More ways to play

Standard chess by default. Opt into Chess960, King of the Hill, or Three-check when your game calls for it.

05 / SEARCH

Built for the hot path

Magic sliding attacks, caller-owned move buffers, fast make/unmake, and a dedicated SearchBoard API.

06 / INTEGRATION

Your application, your UI

Use it in a Go service, a CLI, or an engine. Pair it with chessboard for a browser interface.
