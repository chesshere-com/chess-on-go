LoadFEN always selects standard rules, even on an instance that previously held a variant. Use LoadFENWithVariant whenever loading variant-specific state.

Chess960

NewChess960Game(id) accepts IDs 0–959. ID 518 has the classical back rank. Chess960StartingFEN(id) returns its setup FEN.

Castling finishes on the usual king/rook squares: g1/f1 or c1/d1 for White, and g8/f8 or c8/d8 for Black. FEN() emits Shredder-FEN rook-file rights, such as HAha for ID 518.

SAN uses O-O or O-O-O. UCI input accepts the Chess960 king-to-rook-square form. That explicit form avoids confusing a castle with an ordinary king step that has the same destination. Export PGN to preserve the Variant, SetUp, and FEN tags.

King of the Hill

Standard movement remains in force. A king reaching d4, e4, d5, or e5 wins immediately. Loaded positions are checked too. Insufficient material does not end a King of the Hill game, because reaching the center can still win.

Three-check

Three-check FEN requires a seventh field: +W+B, the checks given by White and Black, from 0 to 3. The initial value is +0+0. Standard FEN loading rejects that extra field.

Check counters survive FEN export, binary serialization, PGN setup tags, and make/undo. A third check produces GameStatusVariantWin; inspect Winner() for the winning side.

PositionKey() includes the active variant, Chess960 rook origins, and Three-check counters. Identical piece placement can have different legal meaning across variants.
